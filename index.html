Why now

California is preparing. Fire season is now year-round, Santa Ana winds are fiercer, and El Niño brings stronger Pacific storms. For a home, the windows and doors are where it all gets in.

Wildfire

Embers, flames and radiant heat arrive together. Heat cracks the glass, and once a window breaks, the fire is pulled inside.

Wind

Santa Ana gusts throw debris at glass. Once a window breaks, fire and weather can get inside.

Coastal storms

Winter storms drive rain and debris at the coast's most exposed windows and glass doors.
