Why now

California is preparing. Longer fire seasons, fiercer Santa Ana winds, stronger Pacific storms and rising water along the coast. For a home, the windows and doors are where it all gets in.

Wildfire

Wind-blown embers travel far ahead of the flames and find their way through gaps and broken glass.

Wind

Santa Ana gusts throw debris at glass. Once a window breaks, fire and weather can get inside.

Storms & rising water

Atmospheric rivers and higher tides are putting more pressure on homes along the coast.
